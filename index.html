10 / FAQ

Questions.
Answered.

Have more questions? Speak to our career team ↗

How can I get started with BhuvixTechnology?

Simply schedule a free strategy consultation through our website. Our career advisors will review your current background, understand your target roles, and recommend the best roadmap forward.

Do I need a four-year degree to work with BhuvixTechnology?

No. BhuvixTechnology values demonstrated skills, verifiable experience, and talent over formal degrees. Alternative credentials, bootcamps, and career switch journeys can successfully unlock U.S. technology roles.

Who is BhuvixTechnology specifically built for?

Our infrastructure is designed for students & fresh graduates, experienced engineers, career switchers, and international talent seeking positions in the U.S. and global technology market.

How long does it typically take to secure a placement?

Most candidates secure client placements within 2 to 4 months, depending on candidate responsiveness, interview readiness, and target role specializations.

Do you support candidates with visa navigation (F1/OPT, STEM OPT, H-1B)?

Yes. We work closely with employer networks and MSP partners experienced in visa sponsorship frameworks, E-Verify compliance, and international talent transitions.
