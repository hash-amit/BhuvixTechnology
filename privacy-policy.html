Privacy Policy.

Last updated: October 2026. How BhuvixTechnology collects, secures, and handles candidate and employer information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when requesting career consultations, submitting resumes, evaluating career tools, or communicating with our career team. This includes your name, email address, phone number, employment history, technical skills, and resume documents.

2. How We Use Candidate Information

Your career data is used exclusively to evaluate job market readiness, rebrand resumes, conduct interview coaching, and introduce your credentials to authorized client employers and Managed Service Provider (MSP) partner networks with your consent.

3. Data Security & Confidentiality

We enforce strict enterprise encryption protocols (TLS/SSL in transit, AES-256 at rest) across all candidate portals. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertising brokers under any circumstances.

4. E-Verify and Government Compliance

As an authorized E-Verify employer agent, BhuvixTechnology verifies candidate employment eligibility in compliance with U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), DHS, and Social Security Administration mandates.

5. Contacting Us

For privacy inquiries or data removal requests, please email privacy@bhuvixtechnology.com or mail our Head Office at Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201309.
