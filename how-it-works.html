Process

From where you are to where you want to work.

A structured 6-stage journey — strategy first, then positioning, skill preparation, targeted job campaigns, interviews and offer support.

Book Consultation ↗
01
STAGE 01 / UNDERSTAND

Career Strategy & Planning

We start with a thorough 1-on-1 discussion to map your current technical stack, aspirations, target location, and visa parameters. We identify competitive salary benchmarks and craft a customized 30/60/90 day execution plan.

02
STAGE 02 / REBRAND

Resume & LinkedIn Profile Rebuilding

Our executive resume writers rebuild your resume from the ground up to achieve 90%+ ATS parsing scores. We optimize your LinkedIn headline, bio, and skill endorsements for recruiter search algorithms.

03
STAGE 03 / PREPARE

Skill Development & Senior Mentorship

Access live technical mentorship with senior engineers working in US enterprises. Master core system design fundamentals, cloud platforms, containerization, and clean architectural patterns.

04
STAGE 04 / CAMPAIGN

Custom Job Campaigns & Direct Outreach

Our recruitment specialists handpick matching client roles and apply on your behalf. We leverage warm contacts with staffing agencies, prime vendors, and Managed Service Providers (MSPs).

05
STAGE 05 / INTERVIEW

Pre-Interview Preparation & Mock Rounds

Before every interview, receive custom company research, role expectations, and practice behavioral STAR questions. After each round, we conduct debriefs to continuously sharpen your responses.

06
STAGE 06 / ONBOARDING

Offer Review, Negotiation & Retention

We evaluate your offer details, negotiate compensation packages, support employment paperwork (I-9/E-Verify), and provide 6–12 months of post-placement career check-ins.
